An intelligent tool that streamlines the process of reaching out to HRs and companies with tailored cold emails. 
Instead of manually drafting emails or constantly switching between resumes, users can simply upload their resumes, list their skills, and paste the job link. 
Llama takes care of the rest—generating a personalized, professional cold email optimized for the job description and your profile. 
Fast, efficient, and recruiter-ready in seconds.
